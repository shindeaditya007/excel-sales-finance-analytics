# 📊 Sales & Finance Analytics Dashboard (Advanced Excel)

## 🔍 Overview

This project delivers a comprehensive **Sales & Finance Analytics solution using Advanced Excel**, enabling data-driven decision-making through dynamic reporting and KPI tracking.

Built using real-world business scenarios, the dashboard analyzes **customer performance, market trends, and financial health** to uncover actionable insights.

---

## 📁 Project Reports

### 📌 Customer Performance Report

Analyzes sales performance across customers to identify growth patterns and high-value segments.
👉 Example: Significant growth observed across multiple customers, with top accounts like Amazon scaling from **12.2M to 82.1M in sales** 

---

### 🌍 Market Performance vs Target

Evaluates country-wise performance against sales targets to identify gaps.
👉 Insight: Overall performance fell short of targets by **~9.17%**, highlighting improvement opportunities in key markets like USA and India 

---

### 💰 Profit & Loss (P&L) – Fiscal Year Analysis

Tracks financial performance across years.
👉 Insight: Net Sales grew from **87.5M (2019) → 598.9M (2021)**, while **Gross Margin % declined slightly**, indicating cost pressure 

---

### 🌐 P&L by Market

Breaks down profitability across global markets.
👉 Insight: Markets like **Japan and New Zealand achieved high GM% (~46–48%)**, while others showed optimization potential 

---

### 📅 P&L by Month & Quarter

Analyzes seasonality and monthly financial trends.
👉 Insight: Consistent revenue growth across quarters with **strong YoY spikes in 2021**, indicating business expansion
